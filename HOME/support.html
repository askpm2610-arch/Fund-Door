Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a crowdfunding campaign?
To create a campaign, sign up for a Fund Door account, click "Start Your Campaign" from your dashboard, and follow our step-by-step guide to set up your project details, funding goals, and rewards.
What are the fees for using Fund Door?
Fund Door charges a 5% platform fee on successfully funded campaigns. Payment processing fees vary by payment method but are typically 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction.
How do I get paid if my campaign succeeds?
Successful campaigns receive payments 14 days after the campaign ends. Funds are transferred to your linked bank account or PayPal after deducting platform and processing fees.
Can I edit my campaign after it's live?
Yes, you can edit most aspects of your campaign while it's live, including the story, rewards, and images. However, you cannot change the funding goal or campaign duration once launched.
What happens if my campaign doesn't reach its goal?
Fund Door operates on an all-or-nothing model. If your campaign doesn't reach its funding goal, no funds are collected, and backers are not charged.
